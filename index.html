Subdomain Takeover Scanner

Checks a domain for CNAME records that point at third-party services which may have been deleted or never claimed. Everything here is passive DNS: CNAME, A and AAAA lookups only. Nothing is requested from the target's web servers.

Authorized use only. Scan domains you own or have written permission to test.

Checks 34 common subdomain names (blog, docs, support, status, shop, pages, and others). Enter the root domain without a protocol or path.
